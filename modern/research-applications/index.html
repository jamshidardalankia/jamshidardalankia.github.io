Research Applications

Portfolio diversification

I implemented a method for grouping stocks using both returns and trading value. In our comparison of 1,000 equally weighted portfolios of ten stocks each, portfolios with similar return-based scores could differ in their exposure to groups identified through trading activity.

Read the portfolio study

Cross-border banking

My coauthors and I compared each country’s share of cross-border banking transactions with its influence in the lending network. In 2018, the United Kingdom had the largest transaction share, while the United States had the greatest weight in the network’s leading eigenvector.

Read the banking study
